Snow Cone Machine — Ice Note

The snow cone machine rental includes all flavour syrups and serving cups, but customers are responsible for providing their own ice. A standard bag of ice from any grocery store works perfectly. We recommend approximately 10 lbs of ice per hour of use for busy events. Questions? Give us a call.

DIY Pickup & Damage Deposit

All snack machine rentals are self-pickup. A fully refundable damage deposit is collected at the time of pickup and returned when the machine comes back clean and in its original condition. Setup and operating guides are included with every rental.
