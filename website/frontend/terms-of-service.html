Terms of Service.

1. Rental Agreement

By booking a rental with Signature Celebrations Event Rentals, the customer agrees to the terms and conditions outlined in this document. The rental period begins at the time of delivery or pickup and ends at the time of return or collection as specified in the booking confirmation.

2. Payment & Deposits

A non-refundable booking deposit is required to secure your event date. The remaining balance is due prior to or at the time of delivery/pickup. For DIY rentals, a refundable damage deposit is required. This deposit will be returned within 48 hours of equipment return, provided the equipment is in its original condition.

3. Cancellation Policy

Cancellations made more than 14 days prior to the event date will result in the loss of the booking deposit. Cancellations made within 7 days of the event date are subject to a fee equal to 50% of the total rental cost. No refunds will be issued for cancellations made within 48 hours of the event.

4. Liability & Safety

The customer assumes all risk and liability for the use of the equipment during the rental period. Signature Celebrations Event Rentals is not responsible for any injuries or damages resulting from the use or misuse of the equipment. Inflatables must be supervised by a competent adult at all times.

5. Damage & Cleaning

Equipment must be returned in the same condition it was received. The customer is responsible for any damage, loss, or theft of the equipment during the rental period. Excessively dirty equipment may be subject to a cleaning fee starting at $50.00.

6. Weather Policy

For the safety of all guests, inflatables cannot be used in rain, high winds (exceeding 25 km/h), or lightning. In the event of predicted inclement weather, we will work with you to reschedule your event or provide a credit for a future rental.
