Frequently Asked Questions.

Q. What areas do you serve?
We are based in Milton, Ontario, and primarily serve Milton, Oakville, Burlington, and Mississauga. Delivery fees vary based on distance. Please contact us for a specific quote for your location.
Q. Is a deposit required for bookings?
Yes, we require a booking deposit to secure your date. For DIY rentals, we also collect a fully refundable damage deposit at the time of pickup, which is returned once the equipment is inspected and confirmed to be in its original condition.
Q. What happens if it rains on the day of my event?
Safety is our top priority. Inflatables cannot be operated in heavy rain or high winds. We offer flexible rescheduling options in the event of inclement weather. Please review our Weather Policy in the Terms of Service for full details.
Q. How much space is needed for a bouncy castle?
Each unit has different dimensions. As a general rule, we require at least 5 feet of clearance around all sides of the inflatable and a clear path for delivery. The setup area must be flat and free of debris or overhead obstructions like tree branches.
Q. Do you provide supervision for the rentals?
Our standard rental rates do not include supervision. The customer is responsible for providing a competent adult attendant to supervise the equipment at all times. Professional attendants can be hired for an additional fee upon request.
