Pick-Up & Party

Save on Delivery.
Keep All the Fun.

Our DIY rental option is the perfect choice for budget-conscious hosts. Pick up your equipment directly from us, follow our easy setup guides, and drop everything off when the party's over. It's that simple.

Every DIY rental includes a fully refundable damage deposit to ensure equipment comes back in the same great condition it left. A deposit is collected at pickup and returned upon safe return.

1
Browse & Book Online

Select your rentals, choose your date, and confirm your booking — all in a few clicks.

2
Pick Up Your Gear

Arrive at our Milton location with your truck or van (required for some larger items — see product pages). Your equipment will be ready and waiting.

3
Set Up & Party

Easy-to-follow setup guides are included with every DIY rental. Your guests will never know the difference.

4
Drop Off & Collect Deposit

Return the equipment in its original condition and we'll refund your damage deposit on the spot.

⚠️ Vehicle Note: Some larger items (e.g., Nerf Artillery Trailer) require a pickup truck or cargo van. Check individual product pages for vehicle requirements before booking.
